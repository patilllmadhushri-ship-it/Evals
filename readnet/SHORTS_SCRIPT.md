# Akshar — Shorts script (~40–45s)

Vertical, fast cuts, no long pauses. One hook, one twist, one close.

---

**[0–5s] HOOK**
**SHOW:** Letters task, five letters on screen: प फ ब भ म. Record a child
mixing up several of them.

**SAY:**
> "I fed a speech AI a recording of five different letters — प फ ब भ म."

---

**[5–12s] THE FAIL**
**SHOW:** zoom in on "Heard (Sarvam AI): प प प प म"

**SAY:**
> "Here's what it transcribed. प. प. प. प. म. Four different letters, and it
> wrote the same one, four times."

---

**[12–25s] THE FIX**
**SHOW:** the Letter check table, margins visible.

**SAY:**
> "So instead of trusting that, we ask the model a narrower question per
> letter: does this sound match what's on screen, or one of the 2–3 letters
> it's usually confused with? That's it — two real mistakes found, correctly,
> from audio a normal transcript completely butchered."

---

**[25–38s] THE TWIST**
**SHOW:** चाँद marked wrong, गाँव marked correct, side by side.

**SAY:**
> "Same idea catches sneakier mistakes too. A child drops a nasal sound —
> चाँद becomes चाद. The speech engine still writes the dictionary word,
> चाँद, like nothing happened. We catch it anyway, straight from the audio.
> And right next to it — गाँव with the same missing sound — we mark
> *correct*. Not a bug. The rulebook says one destroys the word, the other
> doesn't. Every call like that has a written reason."

---

**[38–45s] CLOSE**
**SHOW:** final level + "letters this child got wrong" summary.

**SAY:**
> "End to end: a reading level, and exactly which sounds to practise next —
> built for exactly the kind of speech recognition normally gets wrong."

---

## Cutdown options

**If you need it even shorter (~20s), keep only:**
HOOK + THE FAIL + one line of THE FIX:
> "This speech AI heard four different letters as the same letter, four
> times in a row. So we stopped trusting the transcript and started asking
> the audio directly — did this sound match what's on screen, or not?
> That's how we caught two real mistakes a normal transcript completely
> hid."

**Alternate hook, if चाँद/गाँव tests better with your audience:**
> "Two words, same missing sound. One's marked wrong. One's marked correct.
> That's not a bug — that's the rule that makes this actually trustworthy."
